# Equity & Futures Market Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes the behavior of **equities and equity futures across 8 publicly listed Indian companies spanning multiple sectors**. The analysis compares underlying stocks and futures contracts across different time horizons to evaluate return, risk, liquidity, and futures market structure.

The project was completed as part of coursework at **BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus** and used **Microsoft Excel** extensively for financial-market calculations, comparative analysis, and visualization.

## Objectives

The project aimed to:

- Compare the performance of underlying equities and their corresponding futures contracts.
- Evaluate returns and risk across daily, weekly, and monthly horizons.
- Measure volatility and risk-adjusted performance using the **Sharpe Ratio**.
- Compare liquidity across futures contracts using turnover.
- Analyze futures pricing behavior using **contango and backwardation**.
- Understand how contract characteristics and maturity affect market behavior.

## Analysis Performed

### Equity Performance

For each underlying stock, the analysis evaluated:

- Daily, weekly, and monthly returns
- Average, minimum, and maximum returns
- Standard deviation / volatility
- Risk-adjusted returns
- Sharpe Ratio

The objective was to compare absolute performance with risk-adjusted performance and understand how results changed across different investment horizons.

### Equity Futures

Futures contracts were evaluated across different maturities using:

- Returns
- Volatility
- Sharpe Ratio
- Contract maturity
- Turnover
- Liquidity

The analysis compared futures performance with the corresponding underlying equity to understand differences in risk and return characteristics.

### Futures Market Structure

The project also examined contract specifications and market behavior, including:

- Trading cycles
- Expiry rules
- Contract specifications
- Settlement characteristics
- Maturity structure
- Liquidity across contracts

### Liquidity Analysis

Turnover was used as a measure of futures-market liquidity.

The analysis compared liquidity across different contract maturities to understand how trading activity changes as contracts approach or move further from expiration.

### Contango & Backwardation

Futures prices were compared with spot prices to identify whether contracts exhibited:

- **Contango** — futures prices trading above spot prices.
- **Backwardation** — futures prices trading below spot prices.

This helped evaluate how futures pricing varied across contracts and maturities.
